# Music prompts (AI music generators)

Written 2026-11-23 for Darshan. ChatGPT's first free spins attempt ("Midnight Heist Chase") came out cartoony and thin: 3:05 long, D minor, 144 BPM, quiet (-17.7 dBFS), heavy low end, dull highs. Each prompt below is complete in one paste. Targets were measured from the tracks in the game:
- base game: A minor, 108 BPM, dark and bass-heavy;
- free spins: A minor (the base game's key, so the change feels like the same film), 144 BPM. At that tempo 72 bars = exactly 2:00, a clean loop.

## Free spins: "Night Train Heist" (2:00 loop)

```text
Compose an original, fully instrumental cinematic WESTERN ACTION score cue for a premium slot game's FREE SPINS feature. It must sound like a big-budget film or AAA game trailer score recorded by a real orchestra and session players, NOT cartoon, NOT comedy, NOT children's TV, NOT chiptune.

SCENE: Night. A heavily guarded steam train loaded with government gold races across a moonlit desert. A gang of outlaws on horseback gallops alongside, guns blazing, and jumps onto the wagons to rob it. Danger, speed, grit and heroism - the tone of a dark modern Western action film (think the intensity of a train-robbery set piece), serious and epic.

TECHNICAL SPEC (strict):
- Length: exactly 2:00 (120 seconds).
- Tempo: 144 BPM, constant, 4/4 time, 72 bars. No tempo changes, no ritardando.
- Key: A minor (Phrygian colour allowed: Bb as a tension note). Stay in A minor so the end leads straight back to the start.
- Seamless LOOP: no intro build from silence, no fade-in, no ending, no final chord, no fade-out, no reverb tail into silence. The track starts at full energy on beat 1 and the last bar flows directly back into bar 1 (same groove, same instrument layer as the first bar).
- Instrumental only: no vocals, no choir words, no shouts, no spoken lines.

INSTRUMENTATION (real, recorded, cinematic):
- Driving low strings ostinato in 16th notes (cellos and basses, spiccato, aggressive) that sounds like train wheels on rails.
- Big cinematic percussion: taikos, low toms, snare rolls, anvil hits, frame drums; a galloping rhythm (horses).
- Train sound design WOVEN INTO THE MUSIC, in time with the tempo: rhythmic steam chuffs, metallic rail-joint clacks, wheel rumble, one distant steam whistle every 32 bars. Subtle, under the music, never comic.
- Lead: heroic, gritty brass (French horns and trombones in unison, trumpets for peaks), plus an overdriven, twangy baritone electric guitar answering the brass (spaghetti-western, Morricone-meets-modern-action).
- Colour: harmonica or tremolo electric guitar for short tension moments, low brass stabs, bowed metal or string swells, a sparse wordless male "hey" chant is NOT allowed (no vocals).
- Bass: deep, tight, powerful; modern hybrid low end, but clean, not muddy.

STRUCTURE (bars at 144 BPM):
- Bars 1-8 (0:00-0:13): full-energy start: strings ostinato + percussion + train rhythm, low brass motif.
- Bars 9-24 (0:13-0:40): main theme on horns, guitar answers; the outlaws ride in.
- Bars 25-40 (0:40-1:07): the attack: higher intensity, trumpet peaks, snare rolls, anvil hits, a short gunfight-like percussion break (2 bars).
- Bars 41-56 (1:07-1:33): second theme, darker: tremolo strings, harmonica, guitar lead; the outlaws climb the wagons.
- Bars 57-72 (1:33-2:00): full climax of the main theme, then bars 69-72 drop back to exactly the bar-1 groove so the loop restarts without a jump.

SOUND AND MIX:
- Wide, cinematic, modern blockbuster mix; punchy and loud (around -10 to -12 LUFS), clear high end, tight low end.
- Real instruments and dynamics, natural room reverb, no lo-fi, no MIDI-sounding or toy instruments.

AVOID: cartoon, comedy, slapstick, "Wild West parody", saloon honky-tonk piano, banjo hoedown, oom-pah tuba, pizzicato mickey-mousing, slide whistles, kazoo, xylophone runs, boing sounds, chiptune or 8-bit, children's music, ukulele, happy major-key jingles, cheesy synth brass, thin or quiet mixes, fade-in, fade-out, endings.

OUTPUT: one instrumental track, exactly 2:00, 144 BPM, A minor, seamless loop, WAV or high-quality MP3.
```

## Base game: "Rattlesnake Ridge" (2:00 loop)

```text
Compose an original, fully instrumental cinematic WESTERN score cue for the BASE GAME of a premium slot game. It must sound like a serious big-budget Western film score recorded by real players, NOT cartoon, NOT comedy, NOT children's TV.

SCENE: A lawless desert frontier at dusk. Dust, heat, danger, a lone outlaw watching the railway that carries the gold. Brooding, tense, confident and atmospheric, with a slow-burning groove. It plays for long sessions, so it must stay interesting without becoming tiring.

TECHNICAL SPEC (strict):
- Length: exactly 2:00 (120 seconds).
- Tempo: 108 BPM, constant, 4/4 time, 54 bars. No tempo changes.
- Key: A minor (Bb allowed as a tension note).
- Seamless LOOP: no fade-in, no ending, no fade-out, no reverb tail into silence; the last bar flows directly back into bar 1.
- Instrumental only: no vocals, no choir words, no whistling melody that sounds comic.

INSTRUMENTATION: twangy reverb-drenched baritone electric guitar and nylon acoustic guitar riffs, deep upright or electric bass, brushed snare, stomps and claps, low cinematic toms, soft strings pads, a lonely trumpet or harmonica lead in the middle section, subtle desert wind and a distant rattlesnake as texture.

STRUCTURE: bars 1-8 groove (guitar riff, bass, percussion); bars 9-24 main melody on guitar; bars 25-38 trumpet or harmonica lead, strings swell; bars 39-50 main melody returns fuller; bars 51-54 back to the bar-1 groove for the loop.

SOUND AND MIX: warm, wide, cinematic, around -12 to -14 LUFS (quieter and less busy than the free spins music), clear highs, tight lows, real dynamics.

AVOID: cartoon, comedy, Wild West parody, saloon honky-tonk piano, banjo hoedown, oom-pah, slide whistle, kazoo, xylophone, boings, chiptune, children's music, happy major-key jingles, fade-in, fade-out, endings.

OUTPUT: one instrumental track, exactly 2:00, 108 BPM, A minor, seamless loop, WAV or high-quality MP3.
```

## Follow-ups if a result is off

- Still cartoony: `Too cartoony. Make it a serious dark cinematic action film score: real orchestra, heavy percussion, no comic instruments. Regenerate.`
- Thin or quiet: `The mix is thin and quiet. Make it wide, punchy and loud like a blockbuster trailer, with a clear high end and tight bass.`
- Fades or ends: `Remove the fade-in, the ending and the fade-out. It must loop: the last bar leads straight back into the first.`
- Wrong length: `Make it exactly 2:00 at 144 BPM (72 bars).`
